# PenpalDove

A website that connects people around the world to exchange letters, share stories, and make friends across borders!

## Features

- Create a profile with your interests, languages, and location
- Browse and find penpals who share your interests
- Send and receive messages
- Keep your conversations organized in one place
- Safe and friendly community guidelines
